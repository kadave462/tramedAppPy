Customer Portal

Public-facing, mobile-first page customers use to view their own account — no staff access · Built June 2026

What It Is

The customer portal is a separate, public-facing mini-app that runs inside the same Flask server. Customers access it from their phones — no app to install, no account to create. They just enter the phone number the pharmacy has on file and they're in.

It is completely separate from the internal staff interface. Customers cannot see inventory, other patients, revenue, or any admin data. It is strictly a read-only personal view.

How a Customer Gets In

1
Staff shares the link

Send http://localhost:5000/portal via WhatsApp, print on receipts, or display at counter. When deployed it becomes your real domain, e.g. tramed.rw/portal.

2
Customer enters their phone number

The lookup is flexible — it matches formatted numbers like 0788 123 456 or trailing digits. No password needed.

3
They land on their personal page

Loyalty card, upcoming refills with countdown, full purchase history — all their own data, no one else's.

Routes Added

GET /portal POST /portal Phone Lookup

GET renders the lookup form (portal_lookup.html). POST receives the phone number, searches the customers table with a flexible ILIKE match (also tries last 9 digits for formatted numbers), then redirects to /portal/<id> on success or re-renders the form with an error message.

GET /portal/<customer_id> Customer Portal

Loads the customer, their full sale history (newest first), their loyalty account (if any), and today's date for the refill countdown. Renders portal.html. Returns 404 if the customer ID doesn't exist.

Templates Created

templates/portal_lookup.html

Standalone page (does NOT extend base.html — no staff sidebar). Centred card layout. Shows Pharmacie Tramed logo, phone input, and "View my account →" button. Shows an inline error banner if phone not found.

Mobile viewport: 390px wide, vertically centred.
templates/portal.html

Standalone page. Dark green top bar with customer name and "Sign out" link. Three sections:

  • Loyalty card — shows only if the customer has a LoyaltyAccount. Coloured by tier: amber-700 (Bronze), gray-400 (Silver), amber-500 (Gold). Displays current points balance and lifetime total.
  • Upcoming Refills — all active medication schedules sorted by next_reminder_date. Overdue = red "Refill now" badge. Due within 7 days = amber "In X days" badge. Future dates shown as plain text.
  • Purchase History — last 15 linked sales, date + product names + amount in RWF. Shows "Showing last 15 purchases" footer if there are more.

What Customers Can and Can't Do

Can see
  • ✓ Their loyalty tier and points balance
  • ✓ All active medication schedules
  • ✓ Next refill date per medication
  • ✓ Which medications are overdue
  • ✓ Their own purchase history
  • ✓ WhatsApp contact link to pharmacy
Cannot do
  • ✗ Add or modify schedules
  • ✗ Mark a refill as renewed
  • ✗ See other customers' data
  • ✗ Access inventory or pricing
  • ✗ See revenue or staff reports
  • ✗ Any admin or write action

Design Decisions

Phone number lookup instead of a password

Customers in Kigali predominantly use WhatsApp and don't expect to create accounts at a local pharmacy. The phone number is already in the system. A password would add friction and support overhead for a small team.

Standalone templates (no staff sidebar)

The portal does not extend base.html. This keeps it visually separate from the internal app, prevents any accidental sidebar links showing up, and makes the mobile layout clean with no wasted sidebar space.

Read-only — no writes from the portal

Customers cannot change their schedules. The pharmacist controls medication schedules and the renewal workflow to ensure clinical accuracy. The portal is purely informational.

390px mobile-first width

Most customers will open this on a phone after receiving a WhatsApp link. The layout is designed for a phone screen first — no horizontal scrolling, large tap targets, generous padding.
