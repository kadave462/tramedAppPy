Pharmacie Tramed · Build Notes

Customer Loyalty Module

How it was designed, built, debugged, and what every decision means — June 2026

The Problem We Were Solving

Pharmacie Tramed had no mechanism to reward repeat customers. A patient who buys every month looks identical in Ishyiga to a customer who visited once. There was no incentive built into the system to make loyal customers feel valued or come back faster.

The goal: build a points-based loyalty system that awards points automatically when a customer makes a purchase, notifies them via WhatsApp in Kinyarwanda immediately, and lets staff manage everything from a fast counter screen.

Step 1 — Logic Design (Before Any Code)

The logic was explained and agreed before building. Key decisions made during this phase:

Points earning rate

1 point per 100 RWF spent (base rate). Tier multipliers applied on top. Minimum purchase of 1,000 RWF required — prevents staff awarding points on tiny transactions like a single Paracetamol strip.

Tier structure

Bronze (0–499 pts, ×1.0) → Silver (500–1,999 pts, ×1.5) → Gold (2,000+ pts, ×2.0). Tier based on lifetime points earned, not current balance. This means redemption never causes a tier downgrade — important for customer trust.

Redemption

100 points = 500 RWF discount. Max redemption per sale = 50% of sale value (prevents gaming). Points must be redeemed in multiples of 100 (clean math, easy to explain at counter).

How customers find out they have points

Two ways: (1) Staff tells them verbally after award, (2) Automatic WhatsApp message in Kinyarwanda sent the moment points are awarded. The message shows balance, tier, and how close they are to the next tier.

How points get recorded at the counter

Ishyiga handles the financial transaction (nothing changes there). After the Ishyiga sale is complete, staff opens the Loyalty Counter screen, searches customer by phone, enters the sale amount — points are auto-calculated and awarded in under 10 seconds.

WhatsApp language: Kinyarwanda

The pharmacy's customers are Rwandan. Messages in Kinyarwanda feel personal and local. English tier names (Bronze/Silver/Gold) are kept since they're internationally recognisable brand concepts.

Step 2 — Database Design

Two new tables added to database.py:

loyalty_accounts
  • id
  • customer_id → customers.id (unique)
  • points_balance — spendable now
  • lifetime_points — controls tier, never decreases
  • tier — bronze / silver / gold
  • created_at
loyalty_transactions
  • id
  • account_id → loyalty_accounts.id
  • points — positive=earn, negative=redeem
  • transaction_type — earn/redeem/manual
  • sale_id → sales.id (nullable)
  • note — human-readable description
  • created_at

Key design choice: Two separate point counters — points_balance (goes down when redeemed) and lifetime_points (never decreases). This is what makes tier levels permanent. A Gold customer who redeems 2,000 points doesn't fall back to Bronze. The customer.loyalty relationship uses uselist=False because each customer has exactly one loyalty account.

Step 3 — Building loyalty.py

points_for_amount(amount, tier)

Returns 0 if below 1,000 RWF minimum. Otherwise: int(amount / 100) × tier_multiplier. Integer division ensures clean whole-point awards.

award_points(customer_id, amount_rwf)

Gets or creates the loyalty account. Calculates points. Updates both balance and lifetime. Recalculates tier. Creates a LoyaltyTransaction record. Builds the Kinyarwanda WhatsApp message and wa.me URL. Returns a full result dict. Caller commits the transaction.

_kinyarwanda_message(customer, pts, account, tier_upgraded)

Builds the WhatsApp message text. Always in Kinyarwanda. Shows: greeting (Muraho + first name), thank you, points earned today, total balance, RWF value, tier. If tier just upgraded: shows upgrade celebration. If not Gold yet: shows how many points needed to reach next tier (motivational nudge). Uses WhatsApp bold (*text*) formatting.

Muraho Jean 👋 Murakoze kugura kuri Pharmacie Tramed! ✅ Mwinjiye amanota *45* uyu munsi. 💰 Amanota yanyu: *365 pts* (agaciro: 1,500 RWF) 🏆 Ingereko: *Bronze 🥉* ⭐ Mukeneye *135 pts* gusa kugira ngo mujye ku ngereko ya Silver 🥈! Kugira ngo mukoreshe amanota yanyu, mubwire nimero ya telefoni igihe mukigurana. 📞 0788 301 960 Murakoze cyane! 🙏
redeem_points(customer_id, points, sale_amount)

Validates: account exists, points divisible by 100, balance sufficient, discount ≤ 50% of sale. Deducts from balance only (lifetime stays intact). Creates transaction with negative points.

Step 4 — The Counter Screen UX

The counter screen is the most important UI in this module — it's used by staff at the pharmacy counter, under time pressure with a customer waiting. The design goal was: phone number → points awarded → WhatsApp sent, in under 15 seconds.

Three-step flow (no page reloads — all AJAX):

1
Phone lookup
Staff types phone number, presses Enter. A GET /api/loyalty/lookup?phone=... call returns the customer card (name, tier, balance, progress to next tier). The card colour changes by tier: orange for Bronze, gray for Silver, yellow for Gold.
2
Amount entry with live preview
Staff types the sale total from the Ishyiga receipt. As they type, a live preview updates: "Uzinjira amanota: 45 pts (×1.5 silver bonus)". If the amount is below 1,000 RWF, a Kinyarwanda warning appears. The Award button stays disabled until the amount qualifies.
3
Success + WhatsApp
After award: a success card shows the new balance, RWF value, and the full Kinyarwanda message preview. Two buttons: "Ohereza WhatsApp" (opens wa.me with message pre-filled) and "Client mushya" (resets for next customer). If tier upgraded, a gold celebration banner appears.

Step 5 — Bugs Found and Fixed

Bug 1: multiple values for keyword argument 'TIERS'

loyalty_summary() already returned TIERS in its dict. The route then passed **summary, TIERS=TIERS — two values for the same key. Fix: removed the explicit TIERS=TIERS kwarg since it's already in **summary.

Bug 2: expected token 'end of statement block', got 'ignore'

A leftover draft line in loyalty.html: {% from 'loyalty_macros.html' import tier_badge ignore missing %}. The Jinja2 syntax for optional imports uses ignore missing without quotes but only in newer versions. Removed the line entirely since tier badges are rendered inline.

Step 6 — Seeding Loyalty Accounts

When the DB is seeded fresh, loyalty accounts are auto-created for any customer who has linked sales totalling ≥1,000 RWF. The retroactive points calculation:

total_rwf = sum of all linked sale amounts
base_pts = int(total_rwf / 100)
tier = calculate_tier(base_pts)
pts = int(base_pts × tier_multiplier)

This gives the chronic customers (Jean, Alice, Emmanuel, etc.) a realistic starting balance reflecting their purchase history, so the feature shows real data from day one.
