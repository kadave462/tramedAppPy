Project Build Notes

CS Internship — Digital Transformation & AI Implementation · Updated June 2026

What We're Building

Pharmacie Tramed already runs Ishyiga POS for core transactions — sales recording, stock tracking, and alerts. This project builds the intelligence layer on top: turning raw Ishyiga data into predictions, automation, and insights that increase profitability. Every solution is documented as a replicable blueprint for other pharmacies across Rwanda.

Pages & Routes

GET / Dashboard

Revenue summary cards (today / week / month), % change vs yesterday, 7d/30d/90d revenue line chart, sales-by-category donut chart, low stock alert list, top 5 products by revenue (last 30 days).

GET /inventory Inventory

All 30 products with sell price, cost price, margin %, stock quantity, min level, supplier, and color-coded status (OK / Low Stock / Out of Stock). Search by name, filter by category. Inline stock update modal — click "Update Stock", enter new quantity, save without page reload.

GET /sales Sales History

Paginated list of all sales (25 per page), sorted newest first. Shows date/time, item count, payment method badge (Cash / MoMo / Card), and total. Click any row to expand and see every line item with quantity × unit price.

GET /forecast Demand Forecast

AI-powered reorder intelligence. Summary strip shows count of Critical / Urgent / Soon / OK products. Table shows every product with: days of stock remaining, average daily demand, forecasted demand for next 7 / 14 / 30 days, demand trend (↑↓→), and recommended reorder quantity. "View Chart" opens a modal with 30-day actual sales + 14-day forecast overlay.

Forecasting Model — How It Works

1

Daily aggregation: Pulls all SaleItems for a product over the last 90 days, groups by date, fills missing days with zero to get a complete daily time series.

2

Day-of-week multipliers: Calculates how much each day of the week deviates from the overall mean (e.g. Mondays sell 30% more than average → multiplier = 1.3). Captures weekly pharmacy traffic patterns.

3

Linear trend fit: Deseasonalises the last 30 days of data (removes day-of-week noise), then fits a linear regression using numpy.polyfit. The slope tells us if demand is growing or shrinking over time.

4

Forward projection: For each future day, the model combines trend(day_index) × dow_multiplier, clamped to zero. Produces a 30-day daily forecast array.

5

Reorder logic: reorder_qty = forecast_14d + (avg_daily × 7 safety days) − current_stock. Lead time is 3 days. Urgency is Critical if stock runs out before restock could arrive, Urgent within the safety buffer, Soon within 14 days, OK otherwise.

Database Schema

products
  • id, name, category
  • unit_price, cost_price
  • stock_quantity, min_stock_level
  • supplier_id → suppliers.id
suppliers
  • id, name
  • phone, email
sales
  • id, created_at
  • total_amount
  • payment_method
sale_items
  • id
  • sale_id → sales.id
  • product_id → products.id
  • quantity, unit_price
